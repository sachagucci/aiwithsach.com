# Fatih Nayebi

Fatih Nayebi (Vice President of Data and AI at Aldo Group and lecturer at McGill University's Desautels Faculty of Management) was a guest on AI with Sach, a podcast hosted by Sacha Gucciardo in Montréal.

## Episode 16: Transforming Retail Through AI: Inside ALDO's AI Evolution with Fatih Nayebi (November 2024)

Aldo Group's VP of Data and AI explains how a traditional retailer builds AI teams, forecasting systems, generative AI tools and data foundations, and measures their return.
