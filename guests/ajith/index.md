# Ajith

Ajith (Co-founder, Cactus (Y Combinator batch); previously co-founder of Turing Labs) was a guest on AI with Sach, a podcast hosted by Sacha Gucciardo in Montréal.

## Episode 29: Building Cactus: AI That Actually Helps Solopreneurs (June 2025)

Cactus co-founders explain how a personal need for private chefs led them to build an AI assistant for solopreneurs, and why they build slowly.
