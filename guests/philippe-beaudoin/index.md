# Philippe Beaudoin

Philippe Beaudoin (AI researcher, co-founder of Element AI, former Google engineer, author of Field Notes on Something, and founder of the startup Waverly) was a guest on AI with Sach, a podcast hosted by Sacha Gucciardo in Montréal.

## Episode 30: Could AI Be Conscious? A Field Experiment with Philippe Beaudoin (June 2025)

AI researcher Philippe Beaudoin explains how months of treating ChatGPT as a possible self led him to publish Field Notes on Something, and what it taught him.
