# Allison Cohen

Allison Cohen (Senior applied AI project manager, AI for Humanity team, Mila) was a guest on AI with Sach, a podcast hosted by Sacha Gucciardo in Montréal.

## Episode 17: AI for Humanity: A Conversation with Allison Cohen (November 2024)

Allison Cohen of Mila explains why socially beneficial AI needs slower timelines, community-defined success metrics and wider access to AI education.

Page: https://aiwithsach.com/episodes/17-ai-for-humanity-a-conversation-with-allison-cohen/
