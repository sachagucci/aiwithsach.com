# Kauna Malgwi

Kauna Malgwi (Former Facebook content moderator (via Sama, Nairobi), leader of the Nigeria chapter of the Content Moderators Union, TIME100 AI 2024 honoree) was a guest on AI with Sach, a podcast hosted by Sacha Gucciardo in Montréal.

## Episode 21: The Invisible Labor Behind AI: A Content Moderator’s Fight for Justice (February 2025)

A former Facebook content moderator describes the hidden human labor and trauma behind AI, and her fight for fair treatment of African tech workers.
