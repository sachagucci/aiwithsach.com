# Amogh Chaturvedi

Amogh Chaturvedi (Co-founder, Human Behavior (Y Combinator batch; Stanford dropout)) was a guest on AI with Sach, a podcast hosted by Sacha Gucciardo in Montréal.

## Episode 32: Watching Users So You Don’t Have To: Human Behavior, YC, and AI (June 2025)

A Stanford dropout in Y Combinator explains how his team built an AI that watches session replays, after two rejected ideas and a hundred customer calls.

Page: https://aiwithsach.com/episodes/32-watching-users-so-you-dont-have-to-human-behavior/
