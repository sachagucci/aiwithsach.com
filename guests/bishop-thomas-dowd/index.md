# Bishop Thomas Dowd

Bishop Thomas Dowd (Catholic Bishop of Sault Ste. Marie, Ontario (based in Sudbury); former Ericsson telecom employee and early webmaster) was a guest on AI with Sach, a podcast hosted by Sacha Gucciardo in Montréal.

## Episode 28: A Bishop’s Take on the Age of Intelligent Machines (May 2025)

A Catholic bishop and former telecom worker on AI, trust, loneliness, free will, the soul and regulation.
