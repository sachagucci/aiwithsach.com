# Timothy

Timothy (Co-Founder and Managing Director of the AI Launch Lab; member of the AI Advisory Board at Dawson College) was a guest on AI with Sach, a podcast hosted by Sacha Gucciardo in Montréal.

## Episode 6: Democratizing AI Education: A Conversation with Timothy from the AI Launch Lab (August 2024)

Timothy of the AI Launch Lab on training college students in AI through real projects, who gets shut out of the field, and why AI's current harms matter more than AGI.
