About

AI, honestly. The honest picture of AI: what's true, what's hype, what's at stake.

The show

AI with Sach is a podcast hosted by Sacha Gucciardo in Montréal, recorded in English and in French since July 2024. It talks with the people building AI, questioning it, and living with its impact, including people others don't ask, like a Catholic bishop or a former content moderator.

How we work

  1. True. Facts confirmed by two independent sources. Quotes exact, from the recording. Uncertainty said out loud.
  2. Balanced. Neither hype nor doom. When AI genuinely helps, we say so.
  3. Unseen perspectives. The people others don't ask are part of the honest picture.
  4. A real opinion. Sacha says what he thinks, clearly labeled as his view.
  5. Independent. No paid promotion of AI products, no undisclosed relationships, and public corrections when something was wrong.
