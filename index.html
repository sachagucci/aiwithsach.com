Welcome to AI with Sach

A podcast about what's true in AI, from the people building it, questioning it and living with it.

I'm Sacha Gucciardo, and I host the show from Montréal, in English and in French. I sit down with people who speak from evidence or lived experience: an Element AI co-founder on whether AI could be conscious, a TIME100 AI honoree on the content moderators behind AI, an AI auditor, a Catholic bishop, and the founders, researchers and workers in between.
