# Bias, Rules and Proof

Bias hides in postal codes and training images, and law and audits are catching up. The guests explain what regulation and verification actually look like.

## Episodes

- [Vers une IA Responsable avec Olivier Blais, VP Science Décision chez MoovAI](https://aiwithsach.com/episodes/08-vers-une-ia-responsable-avec-olivier-blais-vp-scie/): Olivier Blais. Olivier Blais of MoovAI explains how to manage AI responsibly: bias, transparency, accountability, ISO standards, and why limiting automation can be the safer choice.
- [L'IA et le droit : Enjeux éthiques, risques et régulation](https://aiwithsach.com/episodes/12-l-ia-et-le-droit-enjeux-ethiques-risques-et-regula/): Céline Castets-Renard, Jade Benson-Poitras. Two law researchers explain how Canada, the EU, the US and China regulate AI, and why existing law already applies.
- [Building Trust Through Responsible AI with Shea Brown (BABL AI)](https://aiwithsach.com/episodes/18-building-trust-through-responsible-ai-with-shea-br/): Shea Brown. Shea Brown of BABL AI explains how independent AI audits work, why companies need them, and where to start with risk assessment.
- [Transforming Ecommerce with Generative AI: Insights from Natalija Pavic from Salesforce](https://aiwithsach.com/episodes/09-transforming-ecommerce-with-generative-ai-insights/): Natalija Pavic. A Salesforce product marketer explains where generative AI is useful in ecommerce today, where it falls short, and why trust and data policy matter.
- [Democratizing AI Education: A Conversation with Timothy from the AI Launch Lab](https://aiwithsach.com/episodes/06-democratizing-ai-education-a-conversation-with-tim/): Timothy. Timothy of the AI Launch Lab on training college students in AI through real projects, who gets shut out of the field, and why AI's current harms matter more than AGI.
- [Will AI Make Universities Complacent? - Nabil Beitinjaneh](https://aiwithsach.com/episodes/10-will-ai-make-universities-complacent-nabil-beitinj/): Nabil Beitinjaneh. A McGill educator explains how AI changes learning, which skills matter, and why job displacement and weak regulation need collective answers.
- [Democratizing Data and AI: Insights from Databricks' Youssef Mrini](https://aiwithsach.com/episodes/14-democratizing-data-and-ai-insights-from-databricks/): Youssef Mrini. A Databricks solutions engineer explains data and AI democratization, governance with Unity Catalog, open source compute, and why compound AI systems beat single models.
