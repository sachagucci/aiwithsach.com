# Who Gets Left Behind

Automation hits the bottom rungs first, and workers across white-collar jobs and entry-level roles lose the way they learned. Guests ask who pays, who trains and who decides.

## Episodes

- [Littératie numérique à l'ère de l'IA : Défis et opportunités pour les employés - Benoit Aubert](https://aiwithsach.com/episodes/11-litteratie-numerique-a-l-ere-de-l-ia-defis-et-oppo/): Benoit Aubert. Benoit Aubert explains why basic digital literacy matters more than AI expertise for most jobs, and why automating entry-level tasks threatens how workers build expertise.
- [Will AI Make Universities Complacent? - Nabil Beitinjaneh](https://aiwithsach.com/episodes/10-will-ai-make-universities-complacent-nabil-beitinj/): Nabil Beitinjaneh. A McGill educator explains how AI changes learning, which skills matter, and why job displacement and weak regulation need collective answers.
- [Democratizing AI Education: A Conversation with Timothy from the AI Launch Lab](https://aiwithsach.com/episodes/06-democratizing-ai-education-a-conversation-with-tim/): Timothy. Timothy of the AI Launch Lab on training college students in AI through real projects, who gets shut out of the field, and why AI's current harms matter more than AGI.
- [How to Revolutionize Data Engineering with Diana, the AI-Powered Jr Data Engineer](https://aiwithsach.com/episodes/05-how-to-revolutionize-data-engineering-with-diana-t/): Josh Gray. Artemis CEO Josh Gray explains Diana, an AI junior data engineer, and argues that AI agents will add to data teams rather than replace them.
- [La Révolution de l'IA avec MoovAI](https://aiwithsach.com/episodes/01-la-revolution-de-l-ia-avec-moovai/): Guillaume Petitclerc. A MoovAI co-founder explains why AI projects fail on adoption rather than technology, and why the first project, governance and human contact matter.
- [The Real Difference Between Data, ML, and AI Engineers](https://aiwithsach.com/episodes/31-the-real-difference-between-data-ml-and-ai-enginee/): Og Ramos. Og Ramos explains what data, ML and AI engineers actually do, why roles are blurring, and why curiosity and fundamentals matter more than any tool.
- [The Creator Economy, EdTech, and AI's Impact with Thomas Fortoul](https://aiwithsach.com/episodes/24-the-creator-economy-edtech-and-ai-s-impact-with-th/): Thomas Fortoul. Thomas Fortoul, host of Thinking Tech, on critical thinking, over-reliance on AI, creators as the new teachers, and education as the lever for the future of work.
