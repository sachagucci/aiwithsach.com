# Hidden Human Costs

Behind every model are data labelers, moderators and communities who were left out. The people who pay are rarely the ones in the room.

## Episodes

- [The Invisible Labor Behind AI: A Content Moderator’s Fight for Justice](https://aiwithsach.com/episodes/21-the-invisible-labor-behind-ai-a-content-moderators/): Kauna Malgwi. A former Facebook content moderator describes the hidden human labor and trauma behind AI, and her fight for fair treatment of African tech workers.
- [AI for Humanity: A Conversation with Allison Cohen](https://aiwithsach.com/episodes/17-ai-for-humanity-a-conversation-with-allison-cohen/): Allison Cohen. Allison Cohen of Mila explains why socially beneficial AI needs slower timelines, community-defined success metrics and wider access to AI education.
- [Democratizing AI Education: A Conversation with Timothy from the AI Launch Lab](https://aiwithsach.com/episodes/06-democratizing-ai-education-a-conversation-with-tim/): Timothy. Timothy of the AI Launch Lab on training college students in AI through real projects, who gets shut out of the field, and why AI's current harms matter more than AGI.
- [AI and Cybercrime: The New Frontier in Digital Forensics](https://aiwithsach.com/episodes/13-ai-and-cybercrime-the-new-frontier-in-digital-fore/): Alain Filotto. A retired RCMP digital forensics expert explains how AI changes fraud, policing and evidence, and why everyone should question what they see online.
- [The Creator Economy, EdTech, and AI's Impact with Thomas Fortoul](https://aiwithsach.com/episodes/24-the-creator-economy-edtech-and-ai-s-impact-with-th/): Thomas Fortoul. Thomas Fortoul, host of Thinking Tech, on critical thinking, over-reliance on AI, creators as the new teachers, and education as the lever for the future of work.
