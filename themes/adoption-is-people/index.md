# Adoption Is People

AI projects rarely fail on the technology. They fail because nobody changed how they work, nobody was consulted, or the problem was never defined.

## Episodes

- [La Révolution de l'IA avec MoovAI](https://aiwithsach.com/episodes/01-la-revolution-de-l-ia-avec-moovai/): Guillaume Petitclerc. A MoovAI co-founder explains why AI projects fail on adoption rather than technology, and why the first project, governance and human contact matter.
- [AI Transformation: Democratizing Innovation at TELUS](https://aiwithsach.com/episodes/19-ai-transformation-democratizing-innovation-at-telu/): Alexandre Guilbault. TELUS AI Accelerator founder Alexandre Guilbault explains how to scale AI adoption: solve business problems first, fail fast, hire for soft skills, prepare for agentic AI.
- [Transforming Retail Through AI: Inside ALDO's AI Evolution with Fatih Nayebi](https://aiwithsach.com/episodes/16-transforming-retail-through-ai-inside-aldo-s-ai-ev/): Fatih Nayebi. Aldo Group's VP of Data and AI explains how a traditional retailer builds AI teams, forecasting systems, generative AI tools and data foundations, and measures their return.
- [Designing the Future of Human Connection — Anick Beaulieu on AI, Creativity & C2 Experiences](https://aiwithsach.com/episodes/27-designing-the-future-of-human-connection-anick-bea/): Anick Beaulieu. The CEO of C2 explains how her events team uses AI as a creative partner while betting on in-person connection, and why IP limits what clients will buy.
- [QuébGPT : Révolutionner les PME avec l’IA – ft Charles Demontigny](https://aiwithsach.com/episodes/22-quebgpt-revolutionner-les-pme-avec-lia-ft-charles/): Charles Demontigny. A Québec founder explains why SMEs struggle with AI, where Québec stands in the AI race, and what his lead-capture chatbot taught him.
- [How to Revolutionize Data Engineering with Diana, the AI-Powered Jr Data Engineer](https://aiwithsach.com/episodes/05-how-to-revolutionize-data-engineering-with-diana-t/): Josh Gray. Artemis CEO Josh Gray explains Diana, an AI junior data engineer, and argues that AI agents will add to data teams rather than replace them.
- [How Salesforce is Using AI to Transform Customer Relationships](https://aiwithsach.com/episodes/03-how-salesforce-is-using-ai-to-transform-customer-r/): Jag. A Salesforce solutions engineer explains how AI is added to CRM, why trust and humans in the loop matter, and why he expects a return to human connection.
