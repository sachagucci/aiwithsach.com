## Summary

Sach talks with Natalija Pavic, who leads alliances strategy for Salesforce Commerce Cloud and holds a patent on generative promotions. She says generative AI is at the "bulletin board" stage of the internet: the technology exists, but the big use cases have not been defined yet. In ecommerce, the current uses are merchant productivity tools, such as generated product descriptions, promotions written from plain-language requests, and copilots, plus chat assistants and natural-language search for shoppers. She explains how her patent grew out of automotive incentives that pulled sales forward without making money. Other topics include optimizing products so GPT recommends them, as businesses once did for SEO, and prompt engineering as a new skill. She also covers the human in the loop and citations as ways to reduce hallucinations. She is critical of image generators that lack diversity and warns that "democratization" of data can hide a loss of privacy. On jobs, she uses the chess example to argue that people who learn to work with AI benefit most. Her advice to companies is to start small, test internally, and tell customers clearly where AI and their data are used. She also says AI behaves like brute force, so data volume and quality matter more than the algorithm.

## Key takeaways

1. Generative AI is early: like the first years of the internet, the major use cases are not defined yet, so experimenting now is useful.
2. Product data quality matters. Clean, complete product attributes and outside mentions help GPT-style assistants recommend your products, as SEO did for Google.
3. Human review and citations are practical ways to keep generated product descriptions on brand and accurate.
4. Be careful with calls to democratize data. Set a privacy policy on who can access what, and tell customers where AI and their data are used.
5. Start with small, low-risk tests such as a landing page or a pop-up. Using the tools also gives you a say, because vendors build what customers pay for.
6. Do not tune the algorithm endlessly. Feed the model more of the right data and put gates in place so it does not find fake correlations.
